Combat prototype

Host or join a session, then click the red capsule near the starting area.
Press 1-5 or click an action to fire. The green circle shows the 10m pistol
range; shots also require an unobstructed line of sight. Dismount first.

1 Quick Shot: Health damage, no HAM cost.
2 Power Shot: greater Health damage.
3 Crippling Shot: Action damage.
4 Disrupting Shot: Mind damage.
5 Burst Fire: damage to all three pools.

The action bar displays Action and Mind costs and the shared cooldown applied
after each action. Those pools regenerate at 4 points per second while all
three player pools are above zero. The server validates and applies attacks.
Successful hits produce a brief spark burst on the side of the dummy facing
the attacker; the effect is shown to all players in the session.
The stationary dummy is defeated when any one pool reaches zero and returns
after eight seconds; its state is shared with the session.

This is a fixed-ability training prototype, not a complete combat system:
there are no weapons or skill requirements, combat queue, enemy attacks,
wounds, battle fatigue, death, or player incapacitation handling yet.
